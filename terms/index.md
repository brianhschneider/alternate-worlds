# Terms of Service — Alternate Worlds publishing software

**Last updated:** September 6, 2026

## 0. Scope

**These terms cover the software that publishes Alternate Worlds clips to social media. They
do not cover the Alternate Worlds newsletter**, which is published on Substack at
[bschneidmtg.substack.com](https://bschneidmtg.substack.com) under Substack's Terms of Use.

## 1. What this is

Software operated by Brian Schneider to publish clips from the Alternate Worlds podcast to
social media accounts he owns. It is not a product, is not offered to the public, and has no
external users.

## 2. Who may use it

The operator. There is no public access, no registration, and no offer to license it.

## 3. Content

Clips are excerpts from interviews recorded with the participants' knowledge. Before
publishing, each clip passes automated checks: the source episode must already have been
released, the person must not appear on a never-publish list, and the clip must not have been
withdrawn from rotation.

Guests may ask at any time for a clip to be changed, taken down, or never used again. See the
Privacy Policy for what is held about guests and how to ask.

## 4. Platform terms

Publishing happens through third-party platform APIs, under each platform's developer terms
and community guidelines. Nothing here overrides them; where they conflict, the platform's
terms govern use of that platform.
